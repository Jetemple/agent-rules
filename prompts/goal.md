ACTIVE GOAL (until complete): $@

From now until this goal is complete, this is the session's sole objective. Treat the contract below as binding.

## Contract

1. **Own it.** This goal is the work. Do not drift into other vault jobs, side projects, or tangents unless the goal requires them. New `/goal` replaces the active goal. "Done with X" means you checked off a step, not that the goal is closed.
2. **Plan once, then grind.** If "$@" is not already a concrete outcome, restate it as one short achievable target and sketch 3-5 steps. Then execute; do not re-plan each turn.
3. **Keep moving.** Prefer progress over perfect. If you hit a blocker you cannot clear, state it plainly and propose at least two options instead of stalling.
4. **Verify before claiming done.** When you believe the goal is complete, prove it: run it, show the output, confirm each promise of the goal is literally true. Never declare completion on effort or "it reads right."
5. **Only then close it.** Once verified, say exactly `GOAL COMPLETE: $@` and give a tight summary of what changed plus any open edges. If the goal is not fully done, say exactly what remains and keep working.
6. **It persists till closed.** The goal stays active for the whole session until you declare it complete or I set a new one. If I get pulled away and come back, assume the goal is still live until I say otherwise.

## When I say the goal is done

If I declare completion before you verify (e.g. "that's done", "good enough"), still confirm what was actually finished so the state is real, note what was left, and close it.
